Founder proof

Study Aloud: where I've exercised the full stack of leadership.

I founded Study Aloud in 2018 — a Bible learning app using adaptive learning to improve biblical literacy — and incorporated it in 2021. As CEO, I spearhead the vision and charge it forward — fostering teams for growth and execution.

That means managing operations, a designer in Nigeria, and a five-person engineering team in Ukraine — meeting regularly across five time zones to keep the work moving. It means architecting the CMS that supports global theology partnerships, leading our infrastructure migration from Google Firebase to AWS in three months, cultivating investor relationships, and allocating the capital we raise.

Every corporate organization I serve gets the benefit of that founder's lens: I see the whole system — product, people, partnerships, and P&L — not just the interface.
