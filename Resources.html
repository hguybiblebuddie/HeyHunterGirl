Reading List

Faith & Theology

  • The Vine Project
    by R.C. Sproul Jr. & Timothy Z. Witmer
    A framework for disciple-making and spiritual development in the context of community.
  • Generous Justice
    by Tim Keller
    How Christian faith calls us toward stewardship, justice, and care for the vulnerable.
  • The Common Good
    by Charles Murray
    Exploring what we owe each other and how faith shapes community responsibility.

Leadership & Systems

  • The Goal
    by Eliyahu M. Goldratt
    A novel about systems thinking, bottlenecks, and the constraints that shape behavior.
  • Radical Candor
    by Kim Scott
    How to care personally while challenging directly as a leader and team member.
  • The Advantage
    by Patrick Lencioni
    How organizational health creates competitive advantage and human flourishing.

Design & Product

  • The Design of Everyday Things
    by Don Norman
    How intentional design shapes human behavior and experience.
  • Inspired
    by Marty Cagan
    How to build products people actually want through discovery and validation.
  • Lean Product Playbook
    by Dan Olsen
    A framework for iterating, learning, and building products that deliver value.

Identity & Becoming

  • Atomic Habits
    by James Clear
    How small systems and identity-based habits compound into transformation.
  • Man's Search for Meaning
    by Viktor Frankl
    Why purpose and meaning matter more than circumstances.
  • The Road Less Traveled
    by M. Scott Peck
    On discipline, grace, and the lifelong journey of becoming.
