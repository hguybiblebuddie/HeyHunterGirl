Devbridge · DFIN · Product Designer · 2020 – 2021

Making SEC compliance feel intuitive — and winning global awards for it.

As a founding designer on DFIN's Active Disclosure, I helped design and launch the B2B SEC reporting platform that went on to win two global Infosec Awards at RSAC 2023.

Objective

Design a complex, compliance-heavy B2B reporting platform that professionals actually want to use.

Outcome

Two global Infosec Awards at RSAC 2023.

Success looked like

A shipped platform trusted for SEC reporting, account management flows that balanced rigor with usability, and design decisions grounded in direct stakeholder research.

Founding-team ambiguity

Joining as one of the founding designers meant there was no established system to inherit — we were defining the platform's design language while shipping it. I specialized in the account management side, where the complexity of roles, permissions, and financial workflows collided with the need for clarity.

Research under regulation

I conducted stakeholder interviews, design reviews, and usability research with the professionals who live inside SEC reporting — aligning every design decision with both product objectives and the unforgiving realities of compliance. The result proved that regulated software doesn't have to feel like punishment.
