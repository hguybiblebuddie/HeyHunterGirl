Pose Direction & Photographer Notes

01
The Power Headshot
  • Chin slightly forward and down (elongates neck, avoids double-chin)
  • Body at 45° angle to camera, face turned toward lens
  • One shoulder slightly raised — creates visual interest
  • Eyes hold steady, let a small natural smile form — "knowing" not "grinning"
  • Hands: one on hip or relaxed at side
  • Backdrop: painted wall, architectural detail, natural greenery
02
The Candid Laugh
  • Photographer tells a bad joke — your real laugh is the shot
  • Eyes crinkle — genuine, not posed
  • Look down then look up (shoot in the middle of the uplook)
  • Hand can go to chest or mouth mid-laugh — natural
  • Walk toward photographer while laughing = movement + joy
  • Golden hour or open shade — no harsh shadows on face
03
The Authority Stance
  • Standing tall, full body in frame — shows your full presence
  • Feet hip-width, slight weight shift to one side
  • Arms: crossed loosely or one hand in pocket
  • Direct eye contact with camera — unwavering, warm, certain
  • Use for speaking page, hero, and anywhere you want "I mean business"
  • Outerwear: blazer, structured jacket, or kimono overlay
04
The In-Motion Shot
  • Walk toward the camera — don't look at lens, look past it
  • Skirt or fabric should be in natural movement
  • Shoot at 1/800 sec minimum to freeze motion + get sharp eyes
  • Mid-stride is the sweet spot — one foot forward, arms naturally swinging
  • Location: open sidewalk, market alley, gravel path, boardwalk
  • Then look back over shoulder for the "follow me" variation
05
Environmental B-Roll
  • Hands tracing a book spine, writing in notebook, holding coffee
  • Detail shots: gold jewelry, curls, bare feet on grass
  • Over-shoulder "working" shot — laptop or journal, looking engaged
  • Silhouette against bright window or open sky
  • Looking out of car window (passenger seat, golden hour driving)
  • Hands holding a mic — even a prop one — confident and natural
06
Light & Technical Notes
  • Golden hour: shoot 60 min before sunset — everything glows warm
  • Open shade: beautiful diffused light, no squinting
  • Avoid midday sun overhead — harsh shadows and squinting
  • Reflector: gold or silver to bounce sun back into face
  • Warm presets in editing — Moody Nude, Brown Sugar, Honey filter vibes
  • Skin should glow, not be corrected out of existence

Must-Capture Shot List — Bring This to Your Photographer

1

The Homepage Hero

Full body or 3/4 length, commanding stance, outdoors, golden light. Clean background so text can overlay. Shoot horizontal (landscape) and vertical both.

2

The About Portrait

Close headshot, relaxed and warm. Looking directly at camera. This one should feel approachable, like you're about to start a real conversation.

3

The Joy Shot

Genuine full laugh, movement, life. Doesn't need to be "pretty" — needs to be real. This is the one people screenshot and share. Get at least 20 burst shots.

4

Podcast / Creator B-Roll

Notebook, pen, thinking pose. Looking away into the distance contemplatively. Over-shoulder desk or outdoor workspace. Mic or headphones if available.

5

The Movement Shot

Walking toward camera, skirt or fabric in motion. Shoot mid-stride. Then do the over-shoulder look back. Use for speaking page and b-roll panels.

6

Detail / Vibe Shots

Close-ups: gold jewelry on wrist, curls in sunlight, hands on a book cover, feet in grass, silhouette against sky. These fill the site's texture sections.

7

Prop Shot — The Car or Bicycle

Against or beside a vintage prop (car, bicycle, old truck). Hand on it, weight shifted, looking right at camera. Relaxed cool. This becomes the most-shared image.

8

The Sunset Silhouette

Just before sun drops below horizon. Arms slightly open, hair catching the light. This becomes the testimonials section background or a full-bleed divider image.
